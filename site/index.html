XRec Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026
Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how hurokume (ふろくめ), the developer of XRec (the "App"), handles your information when you use the App.

1. Overview

XRec records and manages tasks and time records on your device. The App does not provide account registration and does not automatically send your records to servers operated by the developer.

The App does not include advertising or third-party analytics services, and the App itself does not automatically send crash reports. Information may nevertheless be handled through sharing that you initiate, operating system backups, support inquiries, and visits to this page, as explained below.

2. Information Used by the App

The App stores and uses the following information on your device:

  • Task information: Task names, hierarchy, colors, display order, archive status, and related information, used to create, organize, and display tasks.
  • Time records: Start and end times, elapsed time, and related tracking records, used for time tracking, history, summaries, reports, and suggestions for the next task.
  • Plans and preferences: Daily plans, display preferences, notification preferences, and related settings, used to manage plans and retain your choices.
  • Undo history: Information about the state before and after supported changes, including edits and deletions, used to undo those changes.
  • Contents of files you select: Used to import data into the App.

3. Exporting and Sharing Data

When you choose to export or share data, the App creates TSV files containing time records, JSON files containing task information, or PDF reports, and passes them to an app or storage destination you select.

These files may contain task names, time records, and other information included in the export. Storage, use, and further sharing by the selected service are governed by that service's terms and privacy policy.

The developer does not sell the App's record data or provide it to third parties for advertising.

4. Operating System Backups and Device Transfers

Android backup is enabled for the App. Depending on your device settings and operating system, records and preferences may be saved to a backup service provided by Google or another provider, or transferred to another device.

These backups are managed by the operating system or service provider. The developer does not receive or manage them. Please refer to your device settings and the provider's policies for information about backup storage, retention, and deletion.

For Google's services, please see the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications and On-Screen Information

The App uses notifications to display tracking status and provide tracking controls. Notifications may show task names, elapsed time, and suggestions for the next task.

Depending on your device settings, this information may also appear on the lock screen. You can manage its visibility through Android's notification and lock screen settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Records and preferences are stored on your device until you delete them or clear the App's data. There is no fixed retention period for records.

Information that you edit or delete in the App may remain in the undo history, which retains the most recent 100 recorded changes. Older undo entries are removed as new entries are added. Archiving a task does not delete its data.

To remove the App's local data, including undo history and preferences, open Android Settings, go to Apps > XRec > Storage or a similar menu, and select "Clear storage" or "Clear data." Menu names vary by device. Uninstalling the App also normally removes its local app data.

Temporary copies of files created for sharing may remain in the App's cache. You can also remove these copies using Android's "Clear cache" option.

Deleting data in the App or uninstalling it may not remove exported files, copies held by sharing destinations, or operating system backups. Please manage those copies separately at their respective storage destinations. If a backup remains, data may be restored when you reinstall the App or restore your device.

The developer cannot remotely access or delete records stored on your device.

7. Protecting Your Information

The App stores records and preferences in app-specific storage and relies on Android's access controls to protect them. Files that you share and operating system backups are subject to the safeguards of their respective storage destinations.

8. Support Inquiries

If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives the information you provide, such as your email address, name, message, and attachments. This information is used to respond to your inquiry, investigate issues, and maintain a record of the response.

Inquiry information is retained only as long as necessary to handle the inquiry and maintain relevant support records, and is deleted when no longer needed. Gmail is used to send, receive, and store email. Google's handling of this information is governed by its privacy policy.

To request deletion of information you provided in a support inquiry, please use the contact details below. The developer will respond to such requests unless retention is required by law or a similar obligation.

9. Visiting This Page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The developer has not added advertising or analytics tools to this page.

When you visit this page, GitHub may collect information such as your IP address and the date and time of your visit. For details, please see the GitHub General Privacy Statement .

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the App's features or information handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Where appropriate, significant changes will also be communicated in the App or through another suitable method.
